# Photonic & Continuous-Variable Computing

[← All topics](../README.md)

**States:** Optical modes, quadratures, harmonic oscillators, ladder operators, vacuum, Fock, coherent and squeezed states.

**Representations:** Wigner and phase-space descriptions, Gaussian versus non-Gaussian resources, and a brief introduction to GKP grid states.

**Operations:** Phase shifting, displacement, squeezing and beam splitters; Boson Sampling and Gaussian Boson Sampling.

**Illustrations:** Oscillator energy levels, optical-state phase-space plots and Gaussian gate transformations.

**Outcome:** Recognize the building blocks and constraints of optical continuous-variable computation.
