# Quantum Foundations & Gates

[← All topics](../README.md)

**Foundations:** Vector spaces and complex amplitudes; Dirac notation; Hermitian and unitary matrices; eigenvalues; Born-rule measurement, state update, expectation values, Hamiltonians and Schrödinger evolution.

**Qubits:** Bloch-sphere representation, phase, tensor products, separable and entangled states, and Bell-state correlations.

**Operations:** Pauli operators, spin control, Hamiltonian-generated rotations, ZZ couplings, X/H/CNOT/CZ/SWAP and universal gate sets.

**Illustrations:** Measurement and energy-evolution examples, spin in a magnetic field, Bell-state preparation and controlled-gate circuits.

**Outcome:** Understand the state–evolution–measurement framework and trace elementary multi-qubit operations.
