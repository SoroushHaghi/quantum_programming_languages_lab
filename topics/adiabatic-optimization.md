# Adiabatic Optimization

[← All topics](../README.md)

**Theory:** Adiabatic evolution, Hamiltonian interpolation, avoided crossings, minimum spectral gaps, annealing schedules and the Grover-search connection.

**Encoding:** Binary decision variables, Ising interactions, QUBO objectives and constraint penalties.

**Examples:** Four-vertex Max-Cut mapped to ZZ energies; the traveling salesperson problem represented with one-hot city/position variables, route cost and quadratic penalties.

**Engineering constraints:** Coupling locality, extra variables, hardware embedding, system connectivity and spectral-gap scaling.

**Outcome:** Map a combinatorial optimization objective into a Hamiltonian while recognizing mathematical and hardware limitations.
