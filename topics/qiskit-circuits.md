# Qiskit Circuits & Simulation

[← All topics](../README.md)

**Topics:** Building and composing gate-model circuits, controlled gates, simulator backends, transpilation, qubit and bit ordering, measurement shots, counts and finite-sample variation.

**Illustrations:** Entangling circuits, GHZ-state preparation and measurement histograms; DiVincenzo requirements connecting initialization, gates, coherence and measurement to hardware.

**Code:** [GHZ-state Qiskit example](../examples/ghz_state.py).

**Outcome:** Follow a circuit from state preparation to simulated readout and interpret the measurement results.
