# Variational & Hybrid Quantum Algorithms

[← All topics](../README.md)

**Theory:** Parameterized circuits (ansätze), Pauli expectation values, the variational principle and classical optimization of quantum circuit parameters.

**VQE example:** The one-qubit Hamiltonian $H=2X-Z$ and an $R_y(\theta)$ ansatz. The exact reference ground-state energy is $-\sqrt{5}$.

**Broader direction:** Formulate optimization problems with decision variables, Hamiltonians and constraints; evaluate quantum-oriented approaches alongside classical baselines.

**Code:** [One-qubit VQE example](../examples/vqe.py).

**Outcome:** Understand the parameter–energy–optimizer loop and check a small variational calculation against exact diagonalization.
