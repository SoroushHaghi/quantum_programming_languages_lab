# 01 — Foundations of quantum computing

[← Course overview](../README.md) · [Next: Qubits →](02-qubits-gates.md)

**Topics:** Vector spaces, complex amplitudes, matrix operators, eigenvalues, Hermitian and unitary matrices, Dirac notation, the Born rule, quantum-state update after measurement, expectation values, Hamiltonians and Schrödinger time evolution.

**How the course develops:** The first lecture treats a quantum computation as **encode → evolve → measure**. It compares information encodings (discrete versus continuous variables) and computation styles (gate-based, measurement-based and adiabatic evolution).

**Class material:** Matrix and measurement examples, Hamiltonian evolution, and a software overview of Qiskit, PennyLane and D-Wave Ocean.

**Intended outcome:** Be able to read quantum-state equations and identify the role of state preparation, evolution and measurement.
