# 03 — Continuous-variable and photonic computing

[← Course overview](../README.md) · [← Previous](02-qubits-gates.md) · [Next →](04-adiabatic-optimization.md)

**Topics:** Optical quantum modes (qumodes), harmonic oscillators, field quadratures, ladder operators, Fock states, coherent light, vacuum, squeezed states, phase-space/Wigner representations, Gaussian operations and boson sampling.

**How the course develops:** Instead of representing information only with two-level qubits, this lecture studies optical modes and the gates acting on their continuous variables.

**Class material:** Harmonic-oscillator energy levels, illustrations of optical states in phase space, and the effects of phase shifting, displacement, squeezing and beam splitters.

**Intended outcome:** Recognize the main building blocks of continuous-variable quantum information and how they differ from discrete-variable gate circuits.

*PennyLane is mentioned as a software direction, not demonstrated as an independently implemented project here.*
