# 02 — Qubits, entanglement and gates

[← Course overview](../README.md) · [← Previous](01-foundations.md) · [Next →](03-continuous-variables.md)

**Topics:** Qubit normalization and global phase; the Bloch sphere; tensor products; separable versus entangled states; Pauli operators and rotations, spin–magnetic-field control, two-qubit ZZ couplings, CNOT, CZ and SWAP, and universal gate sets.

**How the course develops:** The abstract mathematical model becomes a physical and logical model of quantum gates. Spin in a magnetic field motivates controlled unitary evolution.

**Class material:** Bell-state preparation, Pauli/Hamiltonian gate derivations, controlled-gate circuits and the hardware–software interface.

**Intended outcome:** Trace a simple quantum circuit and explain how multi-qubit gates create correlations that separate quantum states from product states.
